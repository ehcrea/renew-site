Never forget to change a filter again.

Renew reminds you when it's time to replace the things that wear out, like water filters, toothbrush heads and razor blades, and keeps a history of every replacement.

Small app, real peace of mind

Everything you need to keep up with filters, brush heads and blades, and nothing you don't.

Log in one tap

Tap or swipe to mark something as replaced today, or pick another date. The countdown restarts from the day you actually replaced it.

Reminders that fit you

Choose the reminder time, mark items as replaced or snooze them right from the notification, and pick which devices remind you.

See what's due at a glance

A ring for each item shows how much life is left. Overdue and soon-due items are sorted to the top.

History and insights

See every past replacement, how long each one lasted, and the average lifespan.

Syncs with iCloud

Your items stay in sync through your private iCloud account. Prefer to keep it on one device? Turn sync off.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

One app for every screen

Buy once and use Renew on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. A native app on each, kept in sync by iCloud.

New

Made for iPhone Duo

Folded, Renew shows your list on the outer screen. Unfold it and the most urgent item opens right next to the list. Fold it again and you're back to your list.
